# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. The full text at that link applies to all project spaces: issues,
pull requests, discussions, and any event or channel where someone represents the project.

## In short

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique work, not people.
- Do not harass, insult, or demean anyone, including through sexualized language or imagery,
  and do not publish anyone's private information.
- Keep vendor discussions factual. People from SAP, Microsoft, Salesforce, and their
  competitors and customers all contribute here.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers who are involved in a report do not take part
in deciding it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant: correction, warning,
temporary ban, or permanent ban, depending on the severity and history of the behavior.
