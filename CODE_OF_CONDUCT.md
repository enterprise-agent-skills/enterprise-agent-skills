# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. The full text at that link applies to maintainers and contributors
alike, in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any event or channel
where someone represents the project.

## In short

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique work, not people.
- Do not harass, insult, or demean anyone, including through sexualized language or imagery,
  and do not publish anyone's private information.
- Keep vendor discussions factual. Contributors may work for vendors, their competitors, or
  their customers; disclose an affiliation when it is relevant (see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)).

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers, as described in
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). Reports are handled confidentially.

You can also report abuse to GitHub, which accepts reports about users and content that
violate its Community Guidelines or Terms of Service; see
[Reporting abuse or spam](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
The project has one maintainer today, so report to GitHub if a report concerns the maintainer
or if you get no reply within five working days. Once there are several maintainers, those
involved in a report do not take part in deciding it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant: correction, warning,
temporary ban, or permanent ban, depending on the severity and history of the behavior.
